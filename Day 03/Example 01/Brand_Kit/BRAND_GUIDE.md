# NORTHSTAR ADVISORY — Presentation style

Fictional consulting brand for this workshop. The goal is a calm, precise executive presentation with strong hierarchy and purposeful visuals.

## Palette
Midnight navy #10243A: headings, dark slides, primary chart series.
Teal #167D8D: emphasis, second chart series, process highlights.
Champagne gold #C6A66B: small accents and key figures, not small text on white.
Ivory #F7F5F0: main slide background.
Slate #526579: secondary text.
White #FFFFFF: text on navy and clean panels.

## Typography
Preferred: Aptos Display for headings and Aptos for body copy.
Fallback: Arial for both, used consistently throughout.
Use locally available licensed fonts; do not fabricate or download font files.
16:9 widescreen. Cover title 36–44 pt, slide headings 28–34 pt, body 20–24 pt, chart labels at least 16 pt, source notes 10–12 pt.

## Composition
Keep margins of roughly 0.5 inches and align content to a consistent grid. Aim for one main idea per slide and about 35–55 visible words on typical content slides. Move detail into speaker notes. Use generous whitespace and varied layouts appropriate to the material.

## Logo
Use Images/northstar-logo.svg on light slides and Images/northstar-logo-reverse.svg on navy. Preserve proportions. Place in a consistent corner with clear space; recommended width 1.8–2.2 inches. Do not stretch, recolour, or place over busy artwork. Use one logo per slide.

## Backgrounds
Use Images/executive-cover.png for the cover or a key transition, keeping text in the clear left area. It is an AI-generated abstract workshop asset, not a photograph.
For content slides, use a solid ivory or white background. For a short closing statement, a solid midnight-navy background with white type is appropriate. These exact colours are part of the theme and do not require image files.

## Visuals
Prefer meaningful outline icons in navy or teal, native PowerPoint diagrams, and editable charts. Use one coherent icon family. Avoid emojis, clip-art collections, heavy shadows, crowded card grids, and random decorative stock photography.
Use actual supplied data for charts. Label all workshop data as illustrative. Use direct labels where possible, avoid 3D charts, and use zero baselines for bar charts. Never use colour alone to explain a distinction.
Connectors must meet the shapes they link; labels should sit beside the line with clear separation. Test arrows at full slide size.

## Quality check
Inspect every slide after rendering. Correct clipping, overlapping text, awkward line breaks, distorted logos, low contrast, and disconnected arrows. Keep titles, body text, diagrams, and chart labels editable. Embed assets in the final PowerPoint so it works independently of this folder.
