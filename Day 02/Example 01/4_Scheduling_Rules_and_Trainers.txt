HORIZON L&D - SCHEDULING RULES (updated Sep-2026)

Department blackout rules
- Finance: no training in the LAST 3 or FIRST 3 working days of any month (month-end close).
- Sales: no training in the LAST 2 WEEKS of a quarter (target closing).
- Operations: no training on THURSDAYS (peak delivery day). Warehouse staff prefer 7:00-15:00 sessions.
- HR: no restriction.
- Maximum 2 batches per week across the company (trainer capacity).
- Always tell the requester to check public holidays before confirming dates.
